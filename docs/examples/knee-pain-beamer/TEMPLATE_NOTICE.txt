Native PowerPoint adaptation prepared on 2026-10-18 for this medical presentation Skill. Adaptations include a 16:9 canvas, Chinese typography, a split cover with editable journal and presenter fields, two-level headings, source-image placeholders, and removal of footer numbering and navigation. This template adaptation is distributed under CC-BY-SA-4.0. The theme license does not change the rights in scientific articles used as presentation content.

The original LaTeX source archive is preserved unchanged as metropolis-original.zip. Its source headers retain the original copyright notices.
